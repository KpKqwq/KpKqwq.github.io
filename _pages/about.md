I am a PhD student at Northeastern University and a member of the <a href='https://neu-datamining.github.io/'>NEU Data Mining Group</a>. I am advised by <a href='https://neu-datamining.github.io/wangdl.htm'>Prof. Daling Wang</a> and <a href='https://neu-datamining.github.io/cse/fengshi/'>Prof. Shi Feng</a>.

During my master's study at Yangzhou University, I was advised by <a href='https://qiang2100.github.io/'>Prof. Jipeng Qiang</a>.

# Research Interests

My current research focuses on **Efficient and Interpretable Reasoning**. Previously, my research focused on **Multilingual Text Simplification**.
